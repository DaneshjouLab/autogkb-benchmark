## Annotation Citations
### `rs4149056`
**Sentence:** Genotype T/C of rs4149056 (SLCO1B1 c.521T>C) is associated with increased likelihood of statin-related adverse events when treated with atorvastatin in postmenopausal women with an indication for statin therapy per ESC/EAS dyslipidemia guidelines as compared to genotype T/T (Pearson χ2 p<0.001; multivariable OR 7.4, p=0.002).

**Explanation:** In a prospective study of 145 postmenopausal women randomized to atorvastatin or rosuvastatin, adverse events were significantly more frequent in SLCO1B1 T/C vs T/T carriers (overall χ2 p=0.008; post hoc p=0.002), with the association persisting in the atorvastatin subgroup (p<0.001); multivariable analysis confirmed T/C increased adverse event risk versus T/T (OR 7.4, p=0.002).

**Supporting Citations:**
> Eligible participants were postmenopausal women (45–70 years; ≥12 months of amenorrhea) with an indication for statin therapy according to current European Society of Cardiology/European Atherosclerosis Society (ESC/EAS) guidelines [18].

> A statistically significant association was observed between SLCO1B1 genotype and the occurrence of adverse events (Pearson χ^2 = 9.646, df = 2, p = 0.008).

> Post hoc analysis with Bonferroni correction confirmed a significantly higher incidence among T/C heterozygotes compared with T/T homozygotes (Pearson χ^2 = 9.689, df = 1, p = 0.002).

> After stratification by treatment, the association between SLCO1B1 genotype and adverse events remained significant in the atorvastatin group (Pearson χ^2 = 17.286, df = 2, p < 0.001), whereas no significant association was observed in the rosuvastatin group (Pearson χ^2 = 1.325, df = 2, p = 0.52), consistent with the lower number of adverse events in this group.

> The SLCO1B1 T/C genotype was also independently associated with higher risk, conferring a sevenfold increase compared with the T/T genotype (OR 7.4, p = 0.002).
